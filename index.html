surf_helix

a surf course · Source movement, ridden by hand
no personal best yet

A surf ramp is too steep to stand on, so you never land on one — you fall along it, and the game deletes only the part of your velocity going into the face. Gravity pulls you down the slope. Strafing pulls you back up it, and turns the fall into speed. Hold one key, turn the mouse the same way, and do not stop.

THIS WORKS

Hold A and swing the mouse left, or D and swing right — whichever one pushes you up the face you are on. Keep the turn going the whole way down the ramp. When the ramp leans the other way, swap keys.

THIS DOES NOTHING

Holding W. Holding a strafe key without turning. Turning without a strafe key. Jumping — you are not standing on anything, so there is nothing to jump off. Sliding off the low edge is a fall, and falls cost you the clock.

This one needs a mouse and a keyboard. The whole game is turning the mouse a few degrees at a time while holding a strafe key — there is no touch control that can stand in for it.
Original game. The movement reproduces the Source-engine model — friction, ground acceleration, the 45.6° standable-surface threshold and the 30 u/s air wish-speed cap — from public documentation. All geometry, art and code are original; no Valve assets are used.
